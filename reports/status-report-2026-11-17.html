Where we are

The first build of the weekly P&L is on main (PR #24). Snowflake views make the Green and Amber lines for Redruth and Bromborough. The WEEKLY_PL app shows each line with its RAG, source and rule.

Week 45 agrees with the sources. The Green GL lines, STD Ext NSV, and Mtech birds delivered and DOA have a difference of 0.

Gui can now open the app (PR #26). The app has a Next.js security upgrade (PR #25).

The 13 Red lines are not built. For each pilot site, 2 lines are not kill site lines and 2 Amber lines are empty.

Each Amber and Red line now has a ticket. The tickets are grouped by the open decision, not one for each line. Each ticket has an owner.

Three research tickets are closed (PR #38). HCS lamb kill is in Snowflake. Z61 is the production receipt. Snowflake has no weekly FTE.

The first scheduled refresh succeeded at 11:00 UTC today.

Next steps: add the new Gui questions to the questionnaire on PR #23. Merge it. Send it to Gui before the Wednesday meeting on 2026-11-18.

Issues and solutions

Production Tonnage is too high at Redruth. The rule "Z61 less Z62, base unit KG" counts each production stage. At Redruth the value is about 5 to 7 times finished goods output. At Bromborough it drops 10% to 14% of finished goods, which are counted in cases. Solution: #35 decides the rule. The app is not changed yet.
HCS loads some dates twice. 97 dates have two identical loads. Solution: When Kill (lamb) is built, keep only the last load for each date.
Two Amber lines are empty. Materials (OCI) and Substitution have no value for Redruth or Bromborough in weeks 45 and 46. Solution: Unknown. The cause is not checked. #22 can change the OCI accounts.
"Killed Qty" includes TC'd pigs. The week 45 data shows that "Killed Qty" does not take off TC'd pigs. Solution: The app takes TC'd pigs off. Confirm with Westerleigh 2026-11-13 when the data is final (#10).
The views are too slow for the app. One view read takes approximately 20 seconds. Solution: The daily task copies the views to tables. The app data can be up to one day old.
The new app URL did not resolve for 23 minutes. Solution: Suspend the application service, then resume it. The URL resolved 30 seconds later.
15 npm audit findings remain. They are in the snowflake-sdk dependencies, vitest, vite and postcss. Solution: Unknown. They are not addressed.
Two contacts have no Snowflake user. No user was found for Aswani Nune or Tracy Conall. They cannot open the app. Solution: Unknown.
Pork kill is not final on the kill day. When it becomes final is Unknown. The owner is Unknown.
Mtech and Anaplan numbers may differ. Mtech holds production transactions. Anaplan holds planned transactions. A difference is not an error.
