Issues and solutions

Sites do not use one kill rule. In week 45, Dungannon took DOA off. Anwick, Ashbourne and Ballymena reported birds delivered. Solution: All sites use the Dungannon rule. Gui tells the sites.
Anwick left out Sunday 8 Nov. This was 113,004 birds in week 45. Solution: Include late loads. The gap was a manual entry issue.
"All codes" takes off more birds than rejects. The Mtech condemnation codes include part rejects, for example cropping. Solution: Use all codes, and let the reader expand each figure to every code.
Pork kill is not final on the kill day. When it becomes final is Unknown. The owner is Unknown.
One extra pig row. For Westerleigh 2026-11-13, Snowflake has 3,345 rows. The "Pig Purchase Analysis" report shows 3,344 pigs over scale. The cause is not found.
Mtech and Anaplan numbers may differ. Mtech holds production transactions. Anaplan holds planned transactions. A difference is not an error.
