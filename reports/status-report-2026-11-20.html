Where we are

Gui answered most questions in the first form. The intercompany rule, standard freight and the GL accounts now use his answers (PR #58).

Production Tonnage now counts finished goods only (PR #53). The STO rule counts only cross-plant issues (PR #88). The new production tonnage check ties to within 3.1% at both pilot sites (PR #85).

The app has Week by week and Sales margin tabs (PR #60). It runs as VERSION$9.

The meeting with Gui on 2026-11-18 changed the production variance rule. Variances use production recovery, not standard on sales. The source (ZFI28N) is not in Snowflake (#74).

Next steps: triage the 8 new tickets #74–#81 and give them owners. Send questions on ZFI28N to Aswani Nune. Merge PR #47.

Main risk: 43 Amber lines use a default or a "decided for v1" rule. The production variance lines will change when #75 is done. Do not give app values to sites as final figures yet.
