Overheads

Ticket #27

1Which period does the overheads run rate use?

15 overhead lines use a run rate from the GL. Today the app uses the average of the 13 weeks before the P&L week. This is a default, not a decision.

Ticket #27

2Does Depreciation use the same run rate as the other overheads?

Today the sites get Depreciation from the SAP report S_ALR_87012936. That report is not in Snowflake. The GL has a Depreciation P&L group.

Volume

Ticket #35

3Which production stages are in production tonnage?

Redruth and Bromborough record production with SAP movement type Z61. Z61 records each stage, for example sliced bacon and then packed bacon.

In week 45 at Redruth, all stages give 2,495 t of production. Finished goods only give 460 t. Sales are 253 t. All stages together match Arolink total output within about 2%.

Until you answer, the app shows finished goods only (GL account 5000100), tagged Amber.

Why this matters: At Redruth, all stages give about 5 to 7 times finished goods output. We need the quantity that site finance reports.

Ticket #35

4Does production tonnage include finished goods that SAP counts in cases?

Some finished goods are in cases, not kg. At Bromborough, cases are 10% to 14% of finished goods output. Until you answer, the app changes cases to kg with the material net weight.

Ticket #29

5Which rule finds inedible sales?

The app uses COPA sales where the field EDIBLE_FLAG is "Inedible". Intercompany sales are not included. In week 45, Redruth shows 9.2 t of inedible sales. Bromborough shows 0 t.

Kill (lamb)

Ticket #36

7What is the kill definition for lamb?

HCS lamb kill for Wales is in Snowflake. It has one row for each carcass. In 2026 to 16 November, it has 527,634 carcasses. 770 of them have a scale weight of 0 kg or less. These are probably whole condemned carcasses. This is not confirmed.

Why this matters: For pork, kill does not include TC'd pigs. Lamb can use the same idea.

Ticket #36

8Is the HCS site "Wales" the Llanidloes site?

HCS names the site "Wales". An old Hellenic sheep copy names the lamb site Oakley Park, Llanidloes.

Ticket #36

9Who owns the HCS lamb kill export?

HCS sends a lamb kill file to Snowflake every day. Some days are missing, for example 1 to 25 June 2026. The business owner of the file is Unknown.

Why this matters: The team must ask this person to send the missing days and the condemned kg field.
