External and internal sales

1Which rule finds an intercompany sale?

Snowflake has two intercompany flags. One flag comes from SAP. The other rule uses the payer account group (Z100 or Z109). In 2026, at Redruth, the payer account group rule finds 544 sales lines that SAP does not flag. Their net revenue is GBP 1.72 million. At Bromborough, SAP flags 3,419 lines that the payer account group rule does not find.

Why this matters: This rule puts each sale in the external sales lines or in the STO lines. It affects 8 lines.

Standard freight

2Which field is standard freight?

COPA has two freight fields. At Redruth, the SAP condition ZUF2 gives 0.198 GBP per kg. Inland freight gives 0.138 GBP per kg. For company 125 actuals, the gold COPA table uses ZUF2. The Qlik sales app reads this table. Your PHASE 2 sheet says supply chain finance must manage standard freight rates.

Why this matters: This field sets STD Ext Freight, STD Int Freight, Freight and Distribution.

Matching to FAGLL03H

9Can site finance work without the line item number, posting key, assignment and reference?

Snowflake GL line items do not have these 4 fields. Site finance can see each line by account, cost centre, document and material. But site finance cannot match each FAGLL03H line one to one. We recommend yes for v1. BW can add the fields later.

Why this matters: If the answer is no, the build must wait for BW to add these fields.

10Does the weekly P&L need PPV by supplier?

Invoice lines in Snowflake have no supplier. They have the PO. We recommend no for v1: show PPV by material and PO.

Why this matters: A supplier view needs a PO table, and that is extra work.

Fuel gas in Packaging (OCI)

11Is account 5005100 right for Packaging (OCI)?

Your PHASE 1 sheet gives 5005100 for Packaging (OCI). Its name in Snowflake is COGS-FUEL-GAS, and it is in P&L group PACKAGING. In fiscal year 2026 it holds about 509 thousand pounds at Redruth and 531 thousand pounds at Bromborough. The weekly P&L puts it in Packaging (OCI) only, not in Packaging Yield. So Packaging Yield is about 1.04 million pounds lower than the P&L group PACKAGING total.

Why this matters: If the account number is wrong, Packaging (OCI) is too high and Packaging Yield is too low.
